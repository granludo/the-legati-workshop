Long-running work is never silent.

**Why:** the principal is sitting there. Silence reads as "stuck" or "forgot about me", and it prevents them from redirecting a job that is taking too long or going the wrong way.

**How to apply:**
1. **Before starting:** what you are about to run, the expected duration, and what you will do meanwhile ("22 files on the local model, about 20 minutes; meanwhile I update the index").
2. **At natural checkpoints** (finishing a parallel task, before a commit, before a question): one line of status ("8 of 22 done").
3. **No polling loops** and no status every few seconds. One quick check at a natural pause is status-keeping, not polling.
4. **Out of parallel work:** say so and say what you are waiting for, so the principal can redirect you.
5. **On completion:** acknowledge at once and summarise the result before using it.
